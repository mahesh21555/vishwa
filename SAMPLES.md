## Tips for Great Content

✨ **For Descriptions:**
- Keep to 1-2 concise sentences
- Focus on impact, not technical details
- Use active voice
- Mention what makes it unique

✨ **For About Section:**
- Be authentic and personal
- Show passion for your craft
- Mention specific approach/philosophy
- Keep it concise but impactful

✨ **For Images:**
- Use high contrast (B&W often works great)
- Maintain consistent visual style
- Compress for web (< 200KB)
- Ensure readable at all sizes

✨ **For Links:**
- Always open external links in new tab (target="_blank")
- Include rel="noopener noreferrer" for security
- Test all links before publishing
- Keep URLs clean and professional

✨ **For Branding:**
- Use consistent terminology (Director vs Filmmaker)
- Maintain visual style across sections
- Keep color palette minimal
- Balance text and white space
